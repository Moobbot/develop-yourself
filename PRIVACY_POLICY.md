# Privacy Policy – Mindful Shield

*Last updated: 2026-10-18*

## 1. Overview
**Mindful Shield** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a privacy-first browser extension designed to help users curb unwanted adult content habits by promoting self-reflection and self-development.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
- **All data stays on your device**: All user settings, custom keywords, journals, reflection moods, personal goals, and uploaded media are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local` and local `IndexedDB`).
- **No Remote Servers**: We do not operate external tracking servers, analytics endpoints, or remote databases. No personal data, search queries, or browsing history is ever transmitted over the Internet or sold to third parties.

## 3. Permissions Used
- `storage` & `unlimitedStorage`: Used strictly to save your quotes, goals, journals, and local media on your computer.
- `webNavigation`: Used locally on your machine to analyze search URLs against your keyword list. The URL is processed entirely within your browser and never sent anywhere.
- `tabs`: Used to redirect the current active tab to the local intervention page when an adult search is detected.
- `alarms`: Used to handle timed pauses (e.g., 15-minute pause timer).
- `<all_urls>`: Required to detect searches across various search engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo, Cốc Cốc, YouTube, etc.) and direct adult domains.

## 4. Third-Party Services
If you choose to watch built-in or custom YouTube videos within the intervention screen, YouTube's privacy policy applies to the embedded player. We use the privacy-enhanced domain (`youtube-nocookie.com`) for video embedding.
